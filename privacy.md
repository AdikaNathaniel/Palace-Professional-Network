# Privacy Policy — Palace Professional Network

**Effective date:** 11 September 2026
**App:** Palace Professional Network
**Organisation:** International Palace Church (IPC)

Palace Professional Network is a directory and mentorship app built for professionals of the International Palace Church (IPC). This page explains what information the app collects when you register your biodata, how it's used, and who can see it.

## 1. Information we collect

When you register and complete your professional biodata, we collect:

| Field | Required | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Full name | Yes | Identifies you in the directory and chats |
| Phone number | Yes | Your login credential and how other members reach you |
| Age range, gender, marital status | Yes | Shown on your profile |
| Profession / occupation & trade | Yes | Groups you into the right professional network and group chat |
| Place of work | Yes | Shown on your profile |
| Email address | Optional | Alternate contact shown on your profile |
| Profile photo | Optional | Shown on your profile and directory card |

To log in, you also set a 4-digit PIN. We store a one-way **hash** of your PIN, not the PIN itself — we cannot see or recover it.

If you use the chat feature, we store the messages you send (direct messages and profession group chats), tied to your phone number and the recipient or group.

## 2. How we use it

Your information is used only to run the church's professional network:

- Building the searchable directory of IPC professionals
- Matching you to your profession's group chat
- Enabling one-to-one messages between members
- Letting you update your own biodata as your details change (new employer, new phone number, etc.)

**We do not sell or share your information with third parties.** It is not used for any purpose outside the church's professional fellowship activities.

## 3. Who can see it

Your directory profile (name, photo, profession, place of work, phone number, and email if provided) is visible to other *logged-in* members of the app — it is not public on the open internet. Messages you send in a direct or group chat are visible only to the other participant, or to members of that profession's group chat, respectively.

## 4. Storage & security

Biodata and messages are stored in a managed cloud database (MongoDB Atlas); profile photos are stored on the app's backend server. All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/WSS). Access to the directory and chat requires signing in with your phone number and PIN, which issues a time-limited session token.

## 5. Retention & deletion

We keep your biodata for as long as you're an active member of the network, and you can update it yourself at any time from the Biodata Form screen. To request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account and biodata entirely, contact us using the details below.

## 6. Children's privacy

This app is intended for working professionals and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
