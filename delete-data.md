# Delete Your Data — Palace Professional Network

This page covers requesting deletion of **specific data**, without closing your account. If you want your entire account and all data removed instead, see [Delete Your Account](delete-account.md).

## Data you can request to delete individually

| Data | What deleting it does |
|---|---|
| Profile photo | Removed from your directory card and profile |
| Email address | Removed from your profile (optional field to begin with) |
| Chat message(s) | Removed from the relevant direct message or group chat for all participants |
| Full chat history | All of your sent messages are removed from every conversation |

Note: some fields on your biodata — name, phone number, age range, gender, marital status, profession, and place of work — are required to keep your account and directory listing functional. To remove those, request full [account deletion](delete-account.md) instead.

## How to request deletion

Send an email to **[insert privacy contact email]** with:

1. The phone number your account is registered under
2. Which data you want deleted (see table above)
3. The subject line: **"Data deletion request — Palace Professional Network"**

We'll verify the request and complete the deletion within **7 business days**, and reply to confirm once it's done.
