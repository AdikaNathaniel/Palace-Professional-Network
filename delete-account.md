# Delete Your Account — Palace Professional Network

You can request deletion of your Palace Professional Network account and all associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

## What gets deleted

- Your biodata (name, phone number, email, age range, gender, marital status, profession, place of work, profile photo)
- Your login credentials (phone number and PIN hash)
- All chat messages you've sent, in both direct messages and group chats
- Your membership in any group chat

## How to request deletion

Send an email to **[insert privacy contact email]** with:

1. The phone number your account is registered under
2. The subject line: **"Delete my Palace Professional Network account"**

We'll verify the request and permanently delete your account and data within **7 business days**. We'll reply to confirm once it's done.

## What happens after deletion

- You will no longer appear in the professional directory
- Your chat history will no longer be visible to other members
- You will need to register again with a new account if you want to rejoin the network in the future
